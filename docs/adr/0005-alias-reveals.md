# Aliases are only joined to their Subject after their Reveal

Every Alias records the Chapter where the text reveals that it belongs to its Subject (its Reveal). A Summary only uses Mentions made under Aliases whose Reveal is inside the Reader's Spoiler Boundary. An Alias that is visible but not yet revealed acts as its own Subject. Otherwise, looking up one name of a secret identity would pull in Passages under its other names, and the Summary would give away the identity even though every Passage was inside the boundary. Ingestion flags likely secret identities and the admin confirms Reveals in catalogue review. Ordinary names have no Reveal and are joined whenever they are visible, so most Aliases need no attention. A first-appearance default wouldn't work anyway, because "first" depends on the Reading Order. Like everything else, a Reveal is checked against the set-based boundary from ADR 0004, never against a position in a Reading Order.
